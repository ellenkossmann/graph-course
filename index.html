01 · About me

About me

I am an MD-PhD student at the University of Zurich, Center of Experimental Rheumatology. I completed my medical studies at the University of Zurich and hold a Federal Diploma as a physician.

My master's thesis was conducted in the coronary research group at Luzerner Kantonsspital and was published in Cardiovascular Revascularization and Medicine.

02 · About the course

About the course

Generative AI for Work & Research is an 8-week live cohort course by The GRAPH Courses. The course includes live weekly workshops, hands-on practice, personalized feedback and dedicated office hours.

The modules cover documents, presentations and websites; automated data analysis; GenAI for research and text analysis; custom GPTs and chatbots; AI tools for workflow automation; GenAI for communication and outreach; and a capstone project and showcase.

Websites, documents & presentations AI-automated data analysis Text processing & research Custom GPTs & chatbots Task automation Communication & outreach Capstone project & showcase
